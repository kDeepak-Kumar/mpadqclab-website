Brick, block & paver block testing in Hyderabad

Masonry units carry walls and finishes for decades. We check clay bricks, fly ash bricks, concrete and AAC blocks and interlocking pavers against their IS specifications.

Get a quote for this test WhatsApp

Overview

Poor-quality masonry shows up later as cracks, dampness and efflorescence. A few tests at the start of a project help you choose the right supplier and hold them to the specification.

We test samples collected from site or delivered to our lab, and report results against the relevant IS limits for each product.

When you need it

  • Selecting a brick or block supplier
  • Lot-wise acceptance on large projects
  • Paver blocks for driveways, parking and industrial yards
  • Disputes on masonry quality

What you receive

  • Results with IS class / grade compliance
  • Recommendations if units fail or are borderline

Frequently asked questions

How many bricks or blocks are needed for testing?

Typically 10–20 units depending on the tests selected. We confirm the quantity when you share the test list.

Do you test AAC blocks?

Yes. AAC blocks are tested for compressive strength, dry density and drying shrinkage as per IS 2185 Part 3.
