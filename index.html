How we work

From your site to a signed NABL report

One accountable team handles sampling, field testing, laboratory work and engineering review.

  1. 1

    Sample collection at site

    Our technicians visit your site, collect soil, concrete, steel and material samples to IS procedures, and label each one for full traceability.

  2. 2

    Field investigation

    Drilling rigs, SPT, plate load, NDT and pull-out tests are run on site by our own crews and equipment, with no sub-contracting.

  3. 3

    Testing in our NABL lab

    Samples are tested in our ISO/IEC 17025 laboratory on calibrated, computer-controlled equipment.

  4. 4

    Analysis & NABL report

    Engineers review every result and issue a clear report with the NABL symbol and ULR number, ready for your consultant or authority.

FAQs

Questions we're often asked

Is MPAD QC Lab NABL accredited?

Yes. MPAD QC Lab Private Limited is accredited by NABL to ISO/IEC 17025:2017 in the field of testing, certificate number TC-10775. You can verify our status on the NABL website (nabl-india.org) by searching for the certificate number.

Do you collect samples and test at site?

Yes. Our technicians collect samples and carry out field tests (SPT, plate load, NDT, pull-out, field density and more) across Hyderabad and Telangana. You can also deliver samples directly to our laboratory at Hardware Park, Shamshabad.

How quickly will I get the test report?

It depends on the test. Some results are available within days, while others (such as 28-day cube strength or mix designs) depend on curing time. We confirm the turnaround in your quotation and can prioritise urgent work.

Are your reports accepted by government departments and consultants?

NABL is a signatory to the ILAC and APAC mutual recognition arrangements, so NABL-accredited test reports are widely accepted by government departments, consultants, and national and international clients.

How do I get a quotation?

Send an enquiry from this website, WhatsApp us or call +91 90002 19015. Tell us the project location, the tests you need (or the problem you want solved) and the approximate number of samples.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Shamshabad, Hyderabad, and regularly serve Hyderabad, Secunderabad, Rangareddy and the rest of Telangana. For geotechnical investigations we also travel to project sites in other states on request.
