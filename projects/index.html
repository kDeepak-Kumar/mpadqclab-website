Geotechnical

Marine geotechnical investigation

Geotechnical

Borehole & SPT investigation for foundation design

Rotary drilling, Standard Penetration Tests and undisturbed sampling, followed by laboratory soil tests and a safe bearing capacity report.

Field testing

Rebar pull-out (anchorage) testing

On-site hydraulic pull-out tests to verify the bond and anchorage of post-installed reinforcement before concreting.

NDT

NDT condition assessment of RCC piers

Rebound hammer grid testing, supported by UPV and cores, to assess in-situ concrete quality of existing structural members.

Geotechnical

Rock core drilling & logging

Core recovery, RQD logging and labelled core boxes for rock strength and foundation design in hard strata.

Laboratory

Concrete cube testing for project QA

Routine 7- and 28-day compressive strength tests on site cubes in our NABL-accredited laboratory with digital reporting.
