What NABL accreditation means for your project

  • Technically valid results. NABL assesses our people, methods, equipment calibration and quality system against ISO/IEC 17025.
  • Wide acceptance. NABL is a signatory to the ILAC and APAC mutual recognition arrangements, so accredited reports are widely accepted by government departments, consultants and clients in India and abroad.
  • Traceable reports. Results for accredited parameters carry the NABL symbol and a unique ULR number.
  • Continuous oversight. Accreditation is maintained through regular surveillance and re-assessment by NABL.

How to verify our accreditation

  1. Visit the official NABL website: nabl-india.org.
  2. Open the laboratory search and choose Testing laboratories.
  3. Search for certificate number TC-10775 or the name MPAD QC LAB PRIVATE LIMITED.
  4. Check the validity dates and scope shown against the details on this page.
Parameters covered by our NABL scope of accreditation are reported with the NABL symbol and ULR number. Ask us for the current scope document if a test must be NABL-accredited for your submission.
